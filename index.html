Loading the lighting template…

Drop an image straight onto a wall in the frame, or select a surface and paste from the clipboard: Ctrl/⌘+V. In Figma, select the frame and choose Copy as PNG (⇧⌘C). The image stretches over the whole surface, so export it in the surface's proportions; for a sharp 4K PNG of the whole booth 2× is enough (1 px = 1.25 mm), for close-ups export at 4×. Zoom into the frame with Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, a pinch or the + button, drag to move around, double-click to see it all again; Save zoomed view saves that close-up at the full 4K size. Transparent parts of a PNG take the surface colour. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes the last change to the design, Ctrl/⌘+⇧+Z brings it back.
